Cancellation & Refunds.

Draft prepared 17 October 2026. This page is a working outline only; final cancellation and refund terms have not been supplied.

Draft for review: Complete and review this policy before publishing or accepting orders. Do not rely on this page as a final cancellation or refund commitment.

1. Enquiries are not orders

The website prepares a message for WhatsApp. Sending that message begins an enquiry but does not take payment, reserve stock or create a confirmed order. VIRASA will confirm the order and its terms directly.

2. Requesting a cancellation

If you wish to cancel an order, contact VIRASA through the WhatsApp conversation for that order as soon as possible. Whether cancellation is available may depend on whether the order has been configured, produced or dispatched. The applicable decision and any consequences must be confirmed with the customer before the order is accepted.

Eligibility, refund method, amount, timing, and how issues such as an incorrect, damaged or non-functioning product are handled have not yet been confirmed. VIRASA must add the actual process here before launch. Applicable customer rights should be reviewed when preparing the final version.

06GOOD TO KNOW

CLEAR ANSWERS, BEFORE YOU ORDER

Frequently
asked.

Product and supplier details can vary. We’ll confirm any unverified feature with you before accepting an order.

What is an NFC review card?+

It’s a card with an NFC chip and a printed QR code configured to open a business review destination. Customers can tap a compatible phone or scan the code.

How does NFC tapping work?+

On a compatible phone with NFC enabled, hold the phone near the card’s NFC area. A prompt should appear to open the configured link. Phone settings and placement can affect the experience.

Can customers scan the QR code instead?+

Yes. Customers can point their camera app at the printed QR code and follow the link prompt. The QR code is a separate access method from NFC.

Which phones support NFC?+

NFC availability depends on the phone model, region and settings. Check the device maker’s specifications. A QR scan is an alternative where NFC is unavailable.

What if a phone does not support NFC?+

The customer can use the printed QR code with a camera app. They can also open a shared link if your team provides one.

Can I use my own Google review link?+

Yes. Share the HTTPS review or Maps URL you want configured. VIRASA will check the supplied link during setup.

Can I order two different card sizes?+

Yes. The two-card bundle allows two 4×6 cards, two 4×4 cards, or one of each for ₹899 per bundle.

How long does configuration take?+

Timing depends on availability and setup. VIRASA will confirm the expected timing with you before accepting the order.

Is custom branding available?+

Share your requirements in the order form. Custom branding availability and any additional cost need to be confirmed before order acceptance.

Does the dynamic QR service require a subscription?+

The supplier’s service terms and any subscription requirements have not yet been confirmed. VIRASA will clarify this before accepting an order.

Can the QR destination be changed later?+

The QR card is described as dynamic, but the change process, availability and any fees depend on the supplier service and need to be confirmed.

Does changing the QR also change the NFC destination?+

Not necessarily. QR and NFC may have separate destinations and settings. Ask VIRASA to confirm each before ordering or requesting a change.

How do I place an order?+

Complete the order form and continue to WhatsApp. Review the pre-filled message and send it. VIRASA will confirm availability, delivery charges and timing with you. The form does not place or reserve an order by itself.

What happens if the link stops working?+

Contact VIRASA with the card and destination details. Troubleshooting or replacement options depend on the cause and supplier terms, which will be discussed with you.

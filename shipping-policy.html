Shipping & Delivery.

Draft prepared 17 October 2026. VIRASA is based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. Delivery coverage and commercial details still need to be confirmed.

Draft for review: No delivery area, courier, shipping charge or delivery estimate has been confirmed. Do not publish a delivery promise until those details are set.

1. Delivery coverage

VIRASA’s business location is Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. The areas to which orders can be delivered have not yet been confirmed. Ask about delivery to your city or area through WhatsApp before placing an order.

2. Charges and expected timing

Delivery charges and the expected delivery time will be discussed and confirmed by VIRASA before the order is accepted. They may depend on the destination and current fulfilment arrangements. This website does not quote or guarantee shipping rates or delivery dates.

3. Order confirmation and dispatch

Submitting the web form only prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. The customer must send the message and wait for VIRASA to confirm availability, delivery details and the order. The form does not reserve stock or begin dispatch.

4. Delivery details

Customers should provide an accurate delivery city or area and confirm any additional address details directly with VIRASA when an order is discussed. The website does not collect a full street address.

5. Delivery issue

If a confirmed order is delayed, arrives with an issue or cannot be delivered, contact VIRASA through the WhatsApp conversation for that order. The resolution process should be agreed and documented when final delivery arrangements are confirmed.

6. Before publishing

  • Confirm delivery coverage and fulfilment method.
  • Set out how charges are calculated and when they are due.
  • Confirm the expected timing and how customers will be updated.
  • Define a process for failed delivery, damage or a missing parcel, then have the final policy reviewed.

VIRASA · POLICY DRAFT

Privacy Policy.

Draft prepared 17 October 2026. This page explains the intended handling of enquiries on the static VIRASA website. Complete the marked business details and have this draft reviewed before publication.

Draft for review: The legal seller identity, final retention practices, hosting provider and privacy contact have not been supplied. Confirm them before publishing this notice.

1. Who operates this website

This website is for VIRASA, a business based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. The legal name of the operator and a dedicated privacy contact are to be added before launch. For now, a visitor can contact VIRASA through the WhatsApp link shown on the website.

2. What the website collects

The website has no backend order database. Information entered into the product enquiry form stays in the visitor’s browser unless the visitor chooses to continue to WhatsApp. The form may include a name, business name and category, WhatsApp contact number, product and card-size selection, review-page URL, delivery area and optional customisation or message details.

The enquiry form asks for a name, business name, WhatsApp contact number and message. Do not include payment-card details, passwords or other information that is not needed for an enquiry.

3. WhatsApp enquiries

Choosing “Continue to WhatsApp” opens a click-to-chat message addressed to VIRASA. The visitor reviews and sends the message. The website does not send it automatically, place an order, take payment or reserve stock. If the visitor sends it, the message and included information are received and processed through WhatsApp and by VIRASA. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy practices also apply.

4. Cookies, analytics and third parties

The supplied website files do not include analytics, advertising pixels, cookie banners, embedded social feeds or third-party tracking scripts. The page uses local images, CSS and JavaScript. The web-hosting provider may process standard technical logs; identify the selected host and confirm its log practices before publication.

5. How information is used

Information voluntarily sent to VIRASA may be used to answer an enquiry, confirm product availability and configuration, discuss delivery, and manage an order if one is later agreed. Final retention periods, access procedures and any service providers used by VIRASA must be documented before launch.

6. Choices and contact

A visitor can choose not to submit the WhatsApp message. To ask about information already sent to VIRASA, use the WhatsApp contact link on the contact section. Add a dedicated business contact and final process here before publication.

7. Security and changes

The static website does not store submitted details on its own server. Information sent through WhatsApp is handled outside the site. VIRASA should review its business devices and messaging practices for appropriate access controls. This notice should be updated if the site later adds payments, analytics, a backend, cookies or other data collection.

8. Before publishing

  • Add the legal seller identity and a dedicated privacy contact.
  • Confirm the hosting provider’s server-log practices.
  • Document how VIRASA handles and retains WhatsApp enquiries and order details.
  • Have the final notice reviewed for the business’s actual practices and applicable requirements.
